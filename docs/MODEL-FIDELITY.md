# Models and limits

The Observatory is a set of interactive physics models with idealized geometry and illustrative parameters. Each scene states its model; none is an exact reconstruction of a real object.

The equations can be exact within a chosen model while that model omits real effects. Numerical error, model error and an unmeasured result are different things. The checks below are implementation checks, not scientific certification. See the [developer guide](DEVELOPING.md#unattended-test-runs) for commands; an unavailable or unrun check supplies no accuracy evidence.

| Subsystem | Implemented model and assumptions | Domain and limits | Validation status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black-hole image | Numerical Kerr/Schwarzschild null geodesics, with lens-table interpolation and full tracing near table discontinuities. Idealized thin disk or prescribed finite-thickness slab, spectral colour and procedural emissivity. | Finite trace budgets and interpolation limit accuracy. No evolving three-dimensional fluid/magnetic model or returning radiation. Display exposure and accelerated animation are presentation choices. Unresolved display samples remain explicitly uncertain. | Independent quartic/quadrature geometry checks run on the native GPU; full trace versus table is a separate convergence comparison. See the [benchmark procedure](../Tools/benchmarks/README.md) for domains and tolerances. |
| Moving emission and light curves | **Snapshot time retained:** all crossings use the current shared scene/presentation clock, including hot spots and gas. | No path-dependent emission time is integrated. Different lensed images do not show different source ages. Hot-spot curves show frame brightness under this convention; flare curves show the prescribed debris/fallback model. Neither predicts photon arrival delays. | This decision changes labels, not the stationary-emitter shader. Retarded-time/path-delay validation is not applicable to the retained mode; no such validation is claimed. |
| Wormhole | A prescribed Morris–Thorne geometry and ray tracing. | The negative-energy support is hypothetical. The animated pinch is prescribed, not a solution of evolving spacetime or a construction design. | Rendering and readout checks only; no engineering feasibility claim. |
| Warp bubble | A prescribed Alcubierre geometry and relative wall-shape energy display. | The energy dial is relative to this model's default, not a buildable energy budget. Negative-energy support and superluminal control remain hypothetical. | Scene/readout checks, not a realizability result. |
| Relativistic journey | Constant proper-acceleration special-relativistic relations and an ideal photon-rocket mass ratio. | Ideal vacuum travel; no propulsion or thermal engineering solution. Propellant is per tonne of arriving ship plus payload. | Analytic identities and low-rapidity limits are appropriate references; this is distinct from the game's torch drive. |
| Signed mass | Newtonian particles with signed active, passive and inertial mass in the chosen equations. | A runaway pair is a theoretical consequence of this model. Casimir vacuum-energy effects are not negative-inertial-mass objects or interchangeable propulsion fuel. Antimatter is not negative mass. | Numerical conservation checks apply to the toy model, not the existence of its particles. |
| Tides | An equilibrium tide scaled as inverse distance cubed; breakup at the fluid Roche boundary. | No ocean-basin dynamics or material fracture. The historical-date dial extrapolates today's recession rate, rather than reconstructing lunar history. | Local controls/readouts; no regional tide forecast. |
| Mars | Energy balance and illustrative greenhouse, lapse-rate and heat-transport rules. | About 17 displayed years is a fixed relaxation time, not a heat-capacity calculation. No GCM, water inventory or water-vapour runaway feedback. Potential liquid conditions are not actual water coverage. | Scene checks only; not a terraforming forecast. |
| Radio meteors | Line-of-sight forward-scatter geometry with illustrative counts, lifetimes and strengths. | Actual observations depend on antennas, transmitters, receiver and conditions. Tropospheric ducting is a separate effect. | Geometry/readout checks; no predicted observing rate. |

## Reading results

Use a result only for the tested fixture, settings and revision. Record units, reference, tolerance and elapsed simulation time. Separate functional failures from slow performance samples and hang-guard timeouts. A native Unity/GPU run is needed for shader output claims; CPU calculations alone do not verify the GPU. Improving a label does not resolve a separate numerical or mechanics issue.
